## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for a native, civic-first mobile directory serving the municipality of Abu Homs. It balances the dignity of a local municipal authority with the accessibility, warmth, and speed of a modern everyday utility. The visual tone is grounded, civic, and inviting, instilling immediate trust and civic pride while guiding residents and visitors through essential services, commerce, healthcare, and infrastructure.

The aesthetic fuses modern tactile clarity with understated heritage elegance. Pure white surfaces rest gently upon a cool, natural pale green backdrop, accented by layered tones of agricultural emerald and disciplined metallic gold trims. Visual balance is structured around right-to-left flow, where layout anchors, navigation arrows, visual hierarchies, and typographic weight originate deliberately from the right margin.

## Colors

The palette is derived from the fertile landscapes and cultural legacy of Abu Homs, built around rich botanical greens and disciplined editorial neutrals:

- **Canvas & Background (`#F7FAF8`)**: A pale mist green that creates a soft, low-glare surface for sustained outdoor mobile viewing.
- **Primary Emerald (`#0F5132`)**: The foundational institutional shade, applied to app bars, primary action buttons, key brand headers, and critical status badges.
- **Secondary Leaf Green (`#146C43`)**: A mid-tone green used for interactive states, secondary buttons, subheaders, and active toggle selections.
- **Mint Container (`#E6F4EC`)**: Used for soft visual grouping, pill tags, active navigation item backgrounds, and notification badge fills.
- **Gold Accent (`#D4AF37`)**: Reserved for verified badge markers, municipal seals, featured listings, rating stars, and premium community announcements.
- **Surface & Cards (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white reserved for content tiles, card elements, bottom sheets, and modal viewports.
- **Structural Border (`#E4ECE7`)**: A subtle, tinted hairline divider to preserve visual separation without clutter.
- **Text & Content**: Deep charcoal (`#1A241E`) for primary readability, muted olive slate (`#4A5A50`) for metadata and subtitles, and subtle outline gray (`#7B8E83`) for placeholder cues.

## Typography

Typography is calibrated to native Arabic mobile reading ergonomics, using IBM Plex Sans (with support for Arabic Naskh styling). Line heights are expanded by approximately 20–25% relative to Latin proportions to accommodate Arabic ascenders, descenders, and diacritics without clipping.

Typographic hierarchy:
- **Headlines & Banners**: Crisp and authoritative weight (600–700) for rapid scanning during on-the-go searches.
- **Directory Titles & Business Names**: Medium to semi-bold weights (500–600) ensuring distinct clarity against white surfaces.
- **Body & Information Metadata**: Regular weight (400) calibrated with generous line heights (`22px` to `26px`) for phone numbers, street addresses, and service descriptions.
- **Numbers**: Regional Arabic-Indic or Eastern Arabic numerals should render consistently based on user system preference, maintaining proper tabular baseline alignment alongside text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid mobile viewports, structured around a strict 4-column mobile grid system (expanding to 8 columns on foldables and tablets).

- **Margins & Gutters**: Standard horizontal screen padding is `16px` (`margin`), scaling to `20px` (`margin-lg`) on larger devices. Internal card gutter spaces maintain `16px` (`gutter`) to ensure distinct touch targets.
- **RTL Rhythm**: All horizontal layout constraints flow right-to-left: leading icons sit on the right, trailing badges and disclosure chevrons sit on the left, and text aligns natively to the right margin.
- **Vertical Stacking**: Visual sections are partitioned by a `24px` (`space-xl`) cadence to ensure scannable separation between emergency quick-links, sponsored municipal boards, and commercial categories.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft mobile elevation shadows blended with tinted ambient light rather than harsh neutral blacks.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Flat `#F7FAF8` with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Directory Cards, List Rows)**: `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a soft, diffused shadow: `box-shadow: 0px 4px 16px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.05)`. Border outline of `1px solid #E4ECE7` prevents card blend-in on sunny mobile displays.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Actions, Search Header, Sticky Nav)**: Raised surface with `box-shadow: 0px 8px 24px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.09)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets, Dialogs, Emergency Dialers)**: Elevated overlay using `box-shadow: 0px -10px 30px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.12)`, anchored by a soft scrim overlay (`rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a rounded geometric silhouette, rooted at an `18px` (`1.125rem`) radius to soften the utility-heavy nature of civic information.

- **Primary Cards & Containers**: Set to `18px` corner radius.
- **Buttons & Input Fields**: Set to `14px`–`18px` to ensure a consistent, friendly hand-feel.
- **Filter Tags & Status Chips**: Pill-shaped (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical selectors from static card components.
- **Modals & Bottom Action Drawers**: Top right and top left corners rounded to `24px` (`rounded-xl`), creating a smooth bottom-sheet cradle.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#0F5132` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `18px` radius, minimum tap height `48px`. Pressed state shifts to `#146C43`.
- **Secondary Action**: `#E6F4EC` mint fill, `#0F5132` text, `0` elevation, `18px` radius.
- **Icon Buttons (Call, WhatsApp, Maps)**: `44px` circular or `14px` rounded squares with tinted mint or gold accents, supporting immediate one-tap city utility.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF` with a `1px solid #E4ECE7` stroke, `18px` radius, and `48px` minimum height.
- Search icon positioned on the right; voice search or clear action pinned on the left. Active focus transitions border to `#0F5132` with a `2px` soft outer glow (`rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.1)`).

### Directory Cards
- White `#FFFFFF` card body with `18px` corner radius, protected by `1px solid #E4ECE7` and Level 1 elevation.
- Right-aligned business/service imagery or category icon, bold Arabic title, subtle category tag, and horizontal contact action pills docked at the card footer.

### Chips & Filters
- Horizontal scrolling pill chips (`9999px` radius).
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill with `#E4ECE7` border and `#4A5A50` text.
- Active: `#0F5132` fill with `#FFFFFF` text and subtle shadow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular check boxes and radio buttons with `20px` diameter. Selected state features `#0F5132` fill with white glyph check or inner dot; unselected shows `#E4ECE7` border with white core.

### Lists & Navigation
- List groups feature inset horizontal dividers (`#E4ECE7`).
- Native RTL back navigations present a right-pointing chevron (`chevron-right`), indicating return to previous parent view in accordance with RTL spatial orientation.
- Verified local listings receive a dedicated mini gold badge (`#D4AF37`) adjacent to the right of the headline title.
